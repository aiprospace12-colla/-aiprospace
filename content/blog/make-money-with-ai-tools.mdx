**Income potential: $5,000 - $25,000/month**

2. **Content Repurposing Workflows** — Turn one podcast/video into 20 pieces of content. Charge: $800-2,000 setup

## Strategy 3: AI Content Business (Blog + Affiliate)

**Income potential: $1,000 - $10,000+/month**
**Time to first $: 3-6 months**

This is the "slower but scalable" option. You build a content site in a profitable niche, rank on Google, and earn through affiliate commissions.

With AI, you can publish **5x more content** than a traditional blogger. This means you hit the traffic threshold for ad revenue faster and build more affiliate opportunities.

**The math:**
- 100 articles × average 500 monthly readers per article = 50,000 monthly visitors
- $15-20 RPM from display ads = $750-1,000/month from ads alone
- + affiliate commissions at 2-5% conversion = another $1,000-5,000+

**Best niches for AI content in 2025:**
- AI tools (meta, but works)
- Finance and investing
- Health and fitness
- Software reviews
- Home improvement

**Tools:** ChatGPT/Claude for writing, Surfer SEO for optimization, Frase for research
