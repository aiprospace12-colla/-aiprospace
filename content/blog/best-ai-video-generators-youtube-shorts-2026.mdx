## Best Practices for AI-Generated Shorts {#best-practices}

### 1. **Maintain Authenticity and Disclosure**

YouTube's policies require disclosure if your content is significantly AI-generated. This builds trust with your audience rather than diminishing credibility. Many viewers actively support creators leveraging AI efficiently.

### 2. **Customize AI Output**

Never publish raw AI generation without review. Add:
- Your brand's color correction
- Custom intro/outro elements
- Personal commentary or overlays
- Real footage of you when appropriate

### 3. **Focus on Content Quality**

The tool doesn't make the content—the idea does. The best AI video generators simply execute good ideas faster. Great hooks, storytelling, and value propositions matter most.

### 4. **Test Multiple Formats**

AI tools make testing variations affordable. Create 3-5 versions of the same Shorts concept with different hooks, pacing, or angles. YouTube's analytics will show which resonates most.

### 5. **Optimize for Shorts Specifically**

- Vertical format (9:16 aspect ratio)
- Captions on screen (for sound-off viewing)
- First 3 seconds must hook viewers
- Total length 15-60 seconds
- Consistent branding elements

A: Runway's free credits provide the highest quality output, but FlexClip offers the most user-friendly experience for beginners. For pure video quality, Runway's Gen-3 model is superior, but it has a steeper learning curve.

**Q: Do these tools include music and stock footage licenses for YouTube?**

A: Most include royalty-free music and footage libraries. However, verify licenses before uploading publicly. Descript, Synthesia, and Pictory provide fully licensed assets. Always review the tool's terms regarding YouTube monetization specifically.

**Q: How long does it take to create a YouTube Short with AI?**

A: With tools like Pictory AI or FlexClip, 5-15 minutes. With Runway, 15-30 minutes depending on complexity. Professional editing in Descript might take 20-40 minutes if you're recording original content.

**Q: What's the learning curve for these tools?**
